Career
From bedroom to the world stage
2015
"Ocean Eyes" — uploaded on SoundCloud at age 13. Went viral overnight, leading to a record deal.
2017
EP Don't Smile at Me — youngest artist to reach 1 billion streams on Spotify.
2019
Debut album When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? — No. 1 in 20+ countries. "Bad Guy" reaches No. 1 on Billboard Hot 100.
2021
Album Happier Than Ever + Bond theme "No Time to Die" → Oscar for Best Original Song.
2023–24
"What Was I Made For?" (Barbie) wins Oscar & Grammy. Fourth album Hit Me Hard and Soft released.
2026
"Wildflower" wins Song of the Year at the 68th Grammy Awards.
What to say

In 2019, she released her first album which reached number one in more than twenty countries. Since then, she has never stopped — her latest album came out in 2024 and she won Song of the Year at the Grammys in 2026."
Music & style
A unique sound and look
Her music
A mix of pop, alternative and electronic — dark, atmospheric, emotional.
All albums produced with her brother Finneas in a home studio.
Famous for her whispery vocals and lyrics about anxiety, love, and fame.
Inspired by hip-hop artists like Childish Gambino and Tyler, The Creator.
Her style
Famous for oversized baggy clothes — to avoid being judged on her body.
Signature green and black hair became iconic in 2019.
Created her own clothing brand Blohsh in 2018.
Her style constantly evolves — from streetwear to more feminine looks.

"I'm gonna make what I want to make, and other people are gonna like what they're gonna like."
